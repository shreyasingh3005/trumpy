Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our arena rules, ticket pricing, age recommendations, grip socks policy, and birthday parties.

What are your opening hours and where are you located?
+

Trumpy Adventure & Trampoline Park is open 7 days a week from 11:00 AM to 10:00 PM. We are conveniently situated directly opposite Sapphire 83 Mall, Sector 83, Gurugram (near Vatika India Next, with easy access from NH-48 and Dwarka Expressway). Free on-site parking is available for all visitors.

Is there any age or weight limit for jumping?
+

Anyone from age 3 to 60+ years can jump! For little toddlers aged 1 to 6 years, we have a dedicated Toddler Soft Play & Ball Pit Zone where older kids are not permitted, ensuring total safety. For main arena trampolines, our weight limit per jumper mat is 115 kg to maintain optimal spring tension.

Bringing young toddlers? Ask our coordinator on WhatsApp →
Are anti-slip grip socks mandatory, and can I reuse them?
+

Yes, for safety and hygiene, custom silicone grip socks are strictly mandatory for all arena jumpers. Regular socks are slippery and not allowed. Trumpy grip socks can be purchased at the front desk for only ₹99/pair. Best of all, they are washable, high quality, and 100% reusable on all your future visits!

Have Trumpy socks from an earlier visit? Check with our desk on WhatsApp →
What should we wear for our trampoline session?
+

We recommend comfortable athletic wear such as track pants, gym shorts, leggings, and cotton T-shirts. Please avoid clothing with sharp zippers, metal buckles, or loose jewelry that could snag the trampoline netting. Lockers are provided free to secure your shoes, wallet, and watch.

What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?
+

We understand plans with kids can change! You can reschedule your booking free of charge up to 4 hours before your reserved slot by simply sending a message to our WhatsApp support desk (+91 93151 25012). Zero cancellation penalty applies.

Can we bring outside food and drinks into the arena?
+

Outside food and beverages are not allowed inside the park (with the exception of infant formula and custom birthday cakes for booked party suites). Our in-house Trumpy Café serves a delicious range of fresh artisan pizzas, burgers, French fries, hot coffee, shakes, and cold beverages prepared in a hygienic kitchen.

How far in advance should we book a birthday party?
+

Weekend afternoon and evening slots (Saturday & Sunday) fill up very quickly. We recommend booking at least 5 to 7 days in advance to secure your preferred party hall and anchor. Booking 7 days in advance also qualifies you for our Festive 20% OFF Early Bird discount!

+

Yes! For groups of 10 or more guests, we provide tiered corporate and school discounts ranging from 15% to 25% OFF, complete with dedicated tournament anchors, dodgeball referees, customized meal boxes, and GST invoices for easy company expense reimbursement.
