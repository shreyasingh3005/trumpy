Activities Guide

What's Inside Trumpy Arena?

Over 10 active play attractions designed for fitness, thrills, and endless laughter.

⚡ ARENA JUMP

Free Jump & Wall Bounce

Wall-to-wall interconnected high-rebound trampolines with safety padded borders. Jump high, practice flips, and score gravity-defying slam dunks!

🌊 OLYMPIC FOAM PIT

Neon Olympic Foam Pit

Soar into deep soft foam pits illuminated beneath the glowing cyan TRUMPY neon sign. Perfect for practicing aerial somersaults with zero impact.

🚀 HIGH FLYER

Aerial High Zipline

Fast-gliding indoor zipline suspended high over the entire trampoline arena. Feel the wind rush past with certified safety harnesses.

🚲 SUSPENDED CYCLING

Suspended Zip Cycling

Pedal across a high-tension cable suspended in mid-air above the park floor! Gurugram's ultimate thrill ride for daredevils.

🎮 ARCADE & VR

Aliens Extermination & Arcade

High-tech dual light-gun cabinets, racing simulators, claw machines, and VR roller coaster stations for non-stop gaming excitement.

🏏 VR CRICKET

iB Cricket – VR Stadium

Step into a virtual stadium with roaring crowds and smash sixes in world-class VR cricket simulation. Experience true batting physics!

🧗 ROCK CLIMBING

Rock Wall Over Foam Pit

Test your grip strength and agility climbing multi-colored handholds up our vertical bouldering wall before free-falling into foam!

🧸 TODDLER ZONE

Toddler Soft Play Ball Pool

Dedicated sanitised safe zone for ages 1-6 with 20,000+ antibacterial balls, soft foam slides, tunnels, and cute dinosaur obstacle courses.

📸 INSTA-FAMOUS

Glowing Butterfly Photo Wall

Pose at our famous viral illuminated butterfly wings photo booth. Capture breathtaking Instagram reels and profile photos!
