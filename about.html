About Trumpy Adventure Park

Located opposite Sapphire 83 Mall, Sector 83, Gurugram — where physical fitness meets non-stop fun, zero screen time, and unforgettable family memories.

Our Mission

Redefining Indoor Adventure For All Generations

Trumpy Adventure & Trampoline Park was established with a singular vision: to get children, youth, and families off digital screens and into high-energy, physical activity in a safe, hygienic, and air-conditioned environment.

Spanning over 25,000 square feet in Sector 83, Gurugram (right opposite Sapphire 83 Mall), Trumpy is engineered with international-grade jump mats, heavy-duty safety netting, thick impact-cushioning foam pits, and trained safety marshals at every single station.
